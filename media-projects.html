Selected Media Projects

I am proud to have managed the release of the Engish version of "We are Crimea" series for Ukrainer. In these series, created in collaboration with the Ukrainian Institute, our guests revisit Crimea through a VR headset, and share their thoughts and stories about the peninsula they call home.
At Ukrainer I also helped bringing videos about Ukrainian landmarks to an English-speaking audience. This involved managing a wonderful team of translators, proofreaders, and video editors. As a part of this project, we also developed a great linguistic resource - a detailed Ukrainian-English toponym database.
