Navigating Projects and More: My Journey

I am passionate about bringing order to complexity and turning ideas into reality. My path in project management has been anything but linear - it's been an exciting journey of learning, leading, and creating.

My journey started out in web production for a big international company, then I jumped into translation project management where I led a team of five awesome project managers. After that, I worked in multimedia production, which was a totally different world but we have creates some awesome and impactful content. I even stepped in as a programme director during a maternity leave cover, which was a fantastic opportunity to stretch my leadership skills. My project management skills are my secret weapon. I use them pretty much every single day. It's been a wild ride, and I have picked up so many useful skills along the way that help me tackle just about any challenge that comes my way.

My Project Management DNA

Project management isn't just a past career for me. It is a fundamental part of who I am. While I have completed the Google Professional Certificate in Project Management, my real expertise comes from years of hands-on experience across multiple industries. I may not have a formal PMI certification, but I have developed deep, practical skills through leading teams, managing complex projects, and solving real-world challenges.

These days, I channel my project management expertise into organising international research and educational conferences. You could say I am a bit of an organisation enthusiast (okay, maybe a total planning geek). I love bringing structure and vision to complex projects.

My experience in project management has been about more than just completing tasks. I love empowering others as well. I have spent years teaching project management and sharing my insights to help people unlock their potential for effective organisation and leadership. Even though my role has changed, my commitment to helping others develop these crucial skills remains as strong as ever.

What Drives Me

For me, project management isn't about spreadsheets and timelines – it's about:

  • Connecting people
  • Turning complex ideas into achievable goals
  • Creating smooth, efficient workflows
  • Bringing vision to life

Let's Connect
